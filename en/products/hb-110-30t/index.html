Introduction
#

The HB-110-30T CNC Horizontal Boring & Milling Machine is engineered to deliver precision, reliability, and versatility for a wide range of industrial machining applications. This article provides an in-depth look at its features, technical specifications, and available options.

Standard Equipment
#

  • Heidenhain absolute linear scales on X, Y, Z, and W axes
  • Heidenhain absolute angle encoder on B axis
  • Spindle oil chiller
  • Centralized automatic lubrication system
  • Coolant system
  • Air blow system
  • Air conditioner for electrical cabinet
  • Work lamp
  • Chip auger for table
  • M30 automatic power off function
  • Portable MPG handwheel
  • Alarm lamp
  • Tool box with adjustment tools
  • Leveling blocks and bolts

Optional Equipment
#

  • Coolant through spindle (20 bar pressure)
  • Chip conveyor with bucket
  • Spindle support
  • Table splash guard
  • Transformer for voltages other than 220V
  • Right angle milling head
  • Angle plate
  • Oil skimmer
